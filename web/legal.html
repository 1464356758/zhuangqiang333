TierSheet policy drafts

Unpublished functional validation build. Sales are closed. These documents require the seller's real legal name, reachable support email, final terms and approval before a payment provider review or public sale.

Privacy

The app processes product and pricing CSV files in browser memory on your device. It does not upload the files, load external fonts, send analytics or modify a store. Reloading clears the in-memory inputs. Downloaded output stays on your device. Your browser and operating system can retain downloads according to their own settings.

If live checkout is enabled in a future release, clicking the checkout link opens Creem. Creem processes payment, identity, tax and receipt data under its own privacy policy. Product CSV content is not sent to checkout. No API keys are embedded in the application.

Use terms draft

The proposed standalone licence permits the purchaser to use the purchased version for their own business, including generating and sharing their own output documents. It does not permit redistributing the software as a competing paid product. Free WordPress code remains under GPL-2.0-or-later. The proposed purchase includes the purchased version and 12 months of updates; it does not promise lifetime support or a particular commercial result.

The seller supplies a price-list generation tool. You must review the selected source price, discount and overrides before sharing a list. The tool does not convert currencies or determine applicable taxes. PDFs use image-based A4 pages at 150 DPI; HTML and CSV are available for text-based output. Current technical limits are documented in the tutorial.

Refund-policy draft

Proposed: contact the seller within seven days if the product does not fit its advertised formats or use case. Statutory consumer rights and payment provider rules remain applicable. The seller's reachable support email and delivery instructions must be provided before any sale. No live refund process has been tested.
