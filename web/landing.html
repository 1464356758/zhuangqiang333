How it works

1. Choose your product CSV and price rules. 2. Review each tier and any input notices. 3. Download the files and check them before sending.

The tool does not fetch your store, modify products, calculate tax or convert currencies. PDF output uses 150 DPI image pages and installed fonts; long text and very large lists can use CSV or HTML. Outputs are reports for sharing, not store-import files.
